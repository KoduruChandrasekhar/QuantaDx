# User guide

1. **Data**: pick a benchmark, or upload a CSV, a wide genomics CSV or an image ZIP. Nothing is sent until you confirm, and PHI-like columns can be dropped. Explore the profile tabs.
2. **Pre-process**: drag steps to reorder, toggle and configure them, and watch the qubit budget and before/after preview update live. Then apply.
3. **Architecture**: click blocks of the system diagram, play “Follow one patient”, and move the bandwidth slider to watch kernel structure appear.
4. **Models**: select classical and quantum models, configure the circuit, check the resource meters, and run.
5. **Train**: live curves, circuit parameters, parameter heatmap, loss landscape and logs. Deploy the best model when training finishes.
6. **Results**: leaderboard (hover a row to link all charts), O3 scorecard, threshold explorer, significance, generalization, efficiency, noise, bandwidth, calibration, and PDF/CSV export.
7. **Explain**: PCA-aware SHAP, beeswarm, classical-vs-quantum reliance, kernels, a false-negative explorer with waterfall and counterfactuals, and a clinician view.
8. **Predict**: choose an operating mode, drag the risk bands, fill the form (or load an example) and predict. Also batch cohorts and a patient PDF.
9. **API**: try `/predict`, copy snippets, browse the OpenAPI spec.
10. **Deliverables**: verify every official component with “Show me”, or run the guided demo.
